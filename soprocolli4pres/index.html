Bill No. 5 - Bus Etiquette

With the exception of extenuating circumstances, bus riders must:

  • Occupy one seat only. Sitting in the middle, extending your legs and/or arms to two seats prevents others from sitting.
  • If sat on the seat closer to the isle, and someone asks to sit next to you, you must offer either your seat or give ample space for the other to access the window seat.
  • Those sitting on the outside seat are also nonverbally accepting the social contract's sentencing of public shame including audible trash talk directed at them (especially in a non-English language), side eyes, and silent judgement.
  • Those sitting closer to the isle must stand up and exit their seat row if the person next to them needs to leave their chair. Then they can return to their seat.
  • People exiting the bus have the right of way. Bus boarders must wait for those exiting before entering the bus.
